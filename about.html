A two-person software studio in Kashmir.

We build custom software, websites and AI tools for businesses. Both of us work on every project — there is no team behind us to hand your work to.

What we do

Apovix Technologies builds software to order. That covers three kinds of work: custom web applications and internal tools, websites and storefronts, and practical AI automation built into systems people already use.

Every project is scoped, quoted and built by the two of us. We take on a small number of projects at a time, work directly with the people who will use what we build, and stay on after launch. If a project is not a fit for us, we say so in the first call.

You can see what we have shipped on the work section of our homepage.

How we work

Four things we hold to.

Both founders on every project

Nothing is passed down a chain. The people who scope and quote your project are the people who build it.

A build you can open each week

You get something you can click through while it is being built, not a month-long silence followed by a reveal.

Estimates we will stand behind

Scope is agreed in writing before work starts. If something falls outside it, we tell you before we build it, not after.

Code someone else can pick up

Your project should not depend on us being available forever. We hand over code a competent developer can read.
