Users

Who can sign in to this panel.

Two steps to add someone

The website has no public accounts — the only users are the people who administer it. Creating a login and granting it access are separate:

  1. They sign in once. Send them to the sign-in page and have them use Continue with Google. They will be turned away — that is expected — and the page shows their User UID with a copy button. Ask them to send it to you.
    For an email/password account instead, create it in Firebase console → Authentication → Add user and copy the UID from there.
  2. Paste that UID into the form below to grant admin access.

A UID only exists once an account does, which is why they have to sign in before you can grant anything. Creating accounts from this page would need server credentials that a browser cannot hold safely — anything able to do it here could be abused by anyone who opened the page.
