For pharmacies and medical stores

CareVix

Billing, stock and expiry tracking for a pharmacy counter. It runs on the shop's own computer, so it keeps working when the internet does not, and the day's numbers reach the owner's phone.

  • Fast counter billing, with stock updated on every bill
  • Expiry and low-stock warnings before they become losses
  • Customers, receivables and payables in one place
  • Works offline — records stay on your computer, not in the cloud
  • Dashboard on the owner's phone, wherever they are
  • Windows installer, cloud backup, and updates included
