This policy does not override any signed Master Services Agreement or Statement of Work. Where an SOW sets out its own cancellation or refund terms for a project, those terms apply to that project.

1. What this policy is

Apovix Technologies provides custom software development, web development and AI services. Every project is built specifically for one client and cannot be resold or returned to stock.

There is therefore nothing to return and nothing to exchange. This policy deals only with one question: what happens to money already paid if a project stops before it is finished.

2. No money-back guarantee

We do not offer a trial period, a satisfaction guarantee or a blanket money-back guarantee. Once we begin work, our time has been spent and cannot be recovered.

What we offer instead is the warranty in clause 9 of our Terms & Conditions: if a deliverable does not work as the SOW describes, we fix it at no charge.

4. If you cancel the project

You may cancel a project at any time by giving written notice, as set out in clause 17 of our Terms & Conditions. When you do:

  1. We stop work on the date the notice takes effect.
  2. We calculate the value of work completed up to that date, on the basis in section 6.
  3. You pay for that work, plus any third-party costs already committed on your behalf.
  4. Anything you have already paid above that amount is refunded to you.
  5. If the work completed is worth more than you have paid, we invoice you for the difference.
  6. Once the account is settled, we hand over the work completed to that point.

5. If we cancel, or cannot deliver

If we end a project for reasons of our own, or if we are unable to deliver what the SOW describes, you pay only for work you have actually received and accepted. Every rupee paid for undelivered work is refunded in full.

We will hand over the completed work and the source code produced up to that point so that another developer can continue, subject to the intellectual property terms in clause 10 of our Terms & Conditions.

This does not apply where we have stopped work because you have not paid an invoice or have not supplied what clause 6 of the Terms requires. That is a client-side cancellation and section 4 applies.

6. How a refund is calculated

Refunds are calculated against milestones, not against elapsed time. We assess which milestones in the SOW were completed and delivered before the cancellation date, and what proportion of any milestone in progress had been completed.

Deducted from any refund:

  • The value of milestones completed and delivered.
  • A fair proportion of any milestone in progress.
  • Third-party costs already paid or committed on your behalf — domains, licences, paid APIs, hosting.
  • Payment gateway or bank transfer charges already incurred on the original payment.

We will send you a written breakdown of this calculation with the refund decision, so you can see exactly how the figure was reached.

7. What is never refundable

  • Work that has already been delivered and accepted.
  • Third-party costs we have already paid on your behalf.
  • Time spent on discovery, scoping and design that you have received.
  • Fees for a project cancelled because the client stopped responding, after we have followed up in writing.

11. Disputes

If you disagree with a refund decision, write to us and we will review it. Both sides agree to try to settle the matter by discussion first.

If it cannot be settled, the governing law and jurisdiction clause in our Terms & Conditions applies: Indian law, and the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts at Srinagar, Jammu & Kashmir.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our business and the law change. The version published at the time your SOW is signed is the version that applies to that project. Continued use of this website after a change means you accept the updated policy.
